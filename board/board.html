How a score is built

Each job gets sub-scores (0–100) blended with fixed weights. Hover any score to see its breakdown.

Internships
Location 30% · Odds 25% · Résumé value 20% · Fit 15% · Urgency 10%
Part-time
Pay & hours 30% · Résumé value 30% · Odds 20% · Commute 20%

Location: San Diego area and Bay Area 100 · rest of California 70 · remote (US) 60 · rest of the US 30. A posting listing several cities takes its best one. Postings only outside the US are left off.

Fit is category-only for most jobs. The community internship feeds carry no job description, so Fit can't compare the posting to your résumé until it's tailored. Pay shows only when the posting states it; estimates are ignored.
